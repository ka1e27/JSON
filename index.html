Cheat sheet

Know your types

String
"hello" · "42" · ""
Words in quotes. Even "42" in quotes is a string — that's the classic trap.
Number
42 · -7 · 3.14
No quotes, just digits. "The answer to life, the universe, and everything."
Boolean
true · false
A resounding YES, or a load-bearing no. Only two moods.
Null
null
Nothing. Not zero, not empty — genuinely nothing. Like its will to serialize.
Array
[1,2,3] · []
Square brackets. A proud list. "We stand in a line. It's what we do."
Object
{"a":1} · {}
Curly braces with keys. Objects inside objects inside objects…
